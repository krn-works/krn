# LON-133 standards packet

**Standards / pitfalls.** Select only scope/task/branch-relevant pointers; filter before deterministic selection. A digest is disposable, revision/hash-bound context with no authority, capped at 25 lines / 2 KiB; report omissions and expand through explicit search/show. Use one selector for context, digest and bundle relevance. Reject stale, conflicting, wrong-scope, tampered or unsafe inputs. A confined worker reads a read-only snapshot; proposals go elsewhere. Bundle content cannot certify its own applicability or trigger hidden fetches. Qualify fresh-session readback, confinement, cancellation and integrity; byte/line caps alone do not prove safe selection. Use one behavior falsifier, red first, and preserve existing observers.
