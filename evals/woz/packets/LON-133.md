# LON-133 standards packet

**Bounded context.** Select pointers only after scope, task, branch, status, and freshness filters. Preserve stable identity and revision links; show which candidates were omitted for the output budget. A compact digest is disposable context, not authority. Explicit expansion must use the same applicability rules as the digest. Refuse stale, conflicting, wrong-scope, or tampered source material without hidden network access or writes to accepted stores. A worker bundle cannot certify its own relevance. Check byte and line bounds together with selection, provenance, and read-only behavior.
