**D13 amendment (lead under the operator's full autonomy grant, 2026-11-02, t-bz2kwyh):** D13's protection is the context occupancy of one model call, not the lifetime of a worker process. A slice may therefore keep one live worker thread (`krn worker start|send|stop` over `codex app-server`) and send each fix round as a further turn on it, with model and effort passed explicitly per turn. A thread takes at most three turns; before a turn the driver compacts it when the last call's input exceeds half of the model context window or 250k tokens, and above 1M cumulative uncached tokens or before a fourth turn it refuses, and the next round starts a fresh thread with a one-page handoff. Every round's brief keeps the handoff shape. `codex exec --resume` stays limited to interrupted runs and is refused above 500k tokens. Two independent lenses (`.krn/runs/lead/live-worker/lens-architecture.md`, `lens-risk.md`) found that tokens per round are about equal to `exec resume`, because both reuse the cached prefix. The gain is per-turn effort and output schema, interrupting a hung turn instead of killing it, per-turn usage, a reviewed in-repository driver, and path tiers checked on the diff.
